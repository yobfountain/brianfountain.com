GAMES · 2025–2026

Four browser games, all playable today

I design the rules and write the code. Two of these are daily word puzzles. One brings back a board game from 1984. The fourth refuses to be a game at all and gives you a desk to work at instead.

Cryptions2026 · daily word puzzle

Nothing groups by what it says. Each of the sixteen tiles hides a different word behind an anagram, a homophone, an insertion, a deletion, or a string buried inside another string, and the four groups only surface once you decode them. The tile colours carry information too.

Play Cryptions
Cobwebs and Keys2026 · multiplayer board game

A digital recreation of Mystery Mansion, the 1984 Milton Bradley game. Seven chests are hidden in an old house and two hold the real treasure. You build the rooms as you go, search them, and try to carry a chest out the front door. Play alone, or host and hand your friends a four-letter code.

Play Cobwebs and Keys
Cain's Jawbone Reader2026 · reading tool

Edward Powys Mathers printed his hundred-page mystery in the wrong order, and exactly one of the 100! arrangements is right. The reader gives you nothing toward the answer. You read the pages, link the ones that follow each other, tag what you notice, and keep notes on six murderers and six victims. It all stays in your browser and exports as a case file.

Open the reader
Bender2025 · daily word puzzle

A quiet one. Trace words that bend through a grid of letters until every square is used. There are three new puzzles each day, at 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7.

Play Bender
DATA STORY · 2026

Thirty million acres of corn, and what the same land could do with solar

America farms about 30 million acres of corn to make fuel. Corn for Cars asks how much solar it would take to move the same number of vehicle-miles, and the answer is 339,000 acres. That number is the headline, but the argument is carried by maps: county by county, where the corn grows, how much sun falls there, what is happening to the aquifer underneath, and how far it is to the grid.

The model behind it draws on USDA crop data, PVGIS solar estimates, USGS groundwater records and DOE grid data.

TOOLS FOR MAKERS · 2025–NOW

Generative tools for animators, songwriters and novelists

Some of what I build is for other people to make things with. Each of these puts a generative model to work and then keeps a person in charge of the result. The design reasoning for each one is written up at brianfountain.com/work/ai-product-design.

G3NPRO

2025–now · founder and product design lead

A production platform that turns a script into animation. A Director Agent reads each scene, plans the coverage, and writes the shot prompts. A person approves every shot before it ships. Animation studios use it today.

g3npro.com
1K Notes2025–now · music

For people who would never call themselves songwriters. Attach a photo and the model writes an original song from it, melody and lyric both. Every song goes into a public gallery and a radio stream, which is how the next person learns what to try.

1knotes.com
Chapters2025 · fiction

Turns a one-line premise into a novel outline of twenty-seven beats across three acts. A small model runs in the browser by default, so a first draft never leaves the writer's machine. Claude is one toggle away, with the privacy cost stated right beside it.

What carries over

ArmyBakeSale and Cryptions are twenty-three years apart, and both started as an idea that only worked once someone could play with it. I write the rules, the words and the code myself, so the idea and the playable version stay close together. On a team, that means people get something to react to before anyone writes a spec.
